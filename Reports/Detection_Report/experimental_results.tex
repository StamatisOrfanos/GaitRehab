\subsection{Model Accuracy Metrics}
The classification performance across the three gait asymmetry confidence levels—high, moderate, and low—demonstrates consistent trends in model behavior 
and discriminative capacity as seen on the Table \ref{tab:confidence_metrics_clean}. At the high-confidence level, the best overall performance was achieved by 
K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), which yielded accuracy of $0.93$ and a balanced F1-score of $0.95$, closely followed by XGBoost and Random Forest, both of which also 
achieved AUC scores above $0.94$. Notably, all support vector machine (SVM) variants reached perfect precision ($1.00$), but at the cost of lower recall, indicating 
conservative but specific predictions.

At the moderate-confidence level, XGBoost was the top performer with an F1-score of $0.946$ and AUC of $0.958$, closely followed by KNN and Random 
Forest, all of which maintained a strong balance between sensitivity and specificity. Logistic Regression, while achieving high precision ($0.970$), showed 
comparatively lower recall ($0.822$), reinforcing its tendency toward under-detection of asymmetric gait patterns in this setting.

At the low-confidence level, the KNN classifier outperformed other models with the highest accuracy ($0.92$), F1-score ($0.95$), and AUC ($0.95$). 
XGBoost and Random Forest followed closely, demonstrating strong generalization even under noisy or subtly asymmetric conditions.
