\begin{abstract}
Early identification of gait cycle asymmetries plays a critical role in effective stroke rehabilitation and recovery. This study presents 
a novel approach to classify gait cycle symmetry levels using gyroscope signals from shank-mounted inertial measurement units. 
Building upon a previously collected dataset, we segmented the walking data into individual gait cycles, annotated each with a three-tier 
confidence level (high, moderate, low) based on the symmetrical difference of the movements of each leg.
