\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion__future_work}
In this study, we proposed and validated a lightweight machine learning framework for classifying abnormal versus normal gait signals using wearable 
gyroscope data. Our approach relies on just four statistical features—z-axis angular velocity extremes from both shanks—and achieves high classification 
performance across a range of models. The best-performing models demonstrated both high accuracy and perfect AUC under LOOCV, confirming the relevance and 
effectiveness of the selected features. The pipeline’s simplicity and strong performance make it highly suitable for real-world deployment in wearable and 
remote monitoring systems, particularly for early screening or follow-up in rehabilitation programs.

Future work will focus on integrating this classification model into real-time gait monitoring platforms, where it can provide live feedback or alerts during 
walking sessions. Additionally, we plan to evaluate the model in more diverse populations, including post-stroke patients and elderly individuals, and expand 
the feature set to include symmetry and stride variability metrics. These extensions aim to improve generalization and support advanced use cases such as fall 
risk prediction and adaptive rehabilitation.
